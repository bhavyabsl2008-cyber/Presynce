# Attendly Product Domain Analysis & Attendance Intelligence

Attendly represents the traditional attendance and scheduling SaaS product space. Analyzing Attendly allowed us to examine standard academic software patterns, evaluate domain-specific data structures, and identify key usability limitations in existing solutions.

Traditional products like Attendly approach attendance from an administrative logging perspective. Data is presented in static tables, complex percentage grids, and retrospective logs. This approach forces students to perform mental arithmetic to determine how many classes they can safely skip or need to attend to recover lost margin.

Presynce V2 transforms this model into an active intelligence layer. While adopting Attendly's core mathematical principles (such as evaluating attendance ratios against minimum percentage thresholds), Presynce V2 replaces static tabular rows with dynamic freedom statements ("CAN SKIP 3" or "NEEDS 2"). The interface shifts from passive historical logging to real-time decision support and forward-looking projection.
